## Ingredients
- 1 lb hamburger
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1/4 onion, finely minced (optional)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt
- fresh ground pepper
- 1/4 c minced fresh basil (1/8 c dry // 2 Tbsp)
- 1/2 c grated parmesan cheese
- 1 c bread crumbs (panko)
- 1/2 c milk

## Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients (with hands). 
2. Form into golf ball sized meatballs (12). 
3. Bake @ 350 degrees for 30 minutes.
