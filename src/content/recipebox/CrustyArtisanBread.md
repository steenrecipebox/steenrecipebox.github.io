## Ingredients
- 6 1/2 cups flour (2 2/3 wheat to 3 3/4 white; or 2 1/2 wheat to 4 white)
- 4 tsp salt (2 tbspn)
- 1 1/2 tbspn yeast
- 3 cups lukewarm water

## Instructions
1. Mix ingredients (should be sticky and form to bowl). 
2. Cover loosely.  Let sit 2 hours. 
3. If baking, form into loaves (2-3) and/or rolls (24+).  If not baking, put in fridge for up to 14 days.  When ready to use, allow to warm to room temperature then form.  
4. Lightly dust with flour and put on baking sheet with parchment paper. 
5. Rise 40 minutes. 
6. If refrigerated, rise 60 - 90 minutes.  
7. Any unused dough may go in fridge. 
8. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F. 
9. Put water in baking dish on lower rack (to create steam). 
10. Cut tops of bread/rolls. 
11. Bake about 30 minutes (loaves) or 20 minutes if rolls.  
12. Cool on wire rack.
