## Ingredients
- 3 c flour (1 c whole wheat, 2 c all purpose)
- 1 tsp yeast
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 3/4 cups lukewarm water

## Instructions
1. Mix dry ingredients.  
2. Add water.  Will make a wet, loose dough. 
3.  Cover and let sit for 8 to 12 hours. 
4.  Preheat oven to 450 degrees with ditch oven and lid inside. 
5.  Pour dough onto parchment paper.  
6.  Sprinkle with salt and pepper. 
7.  Lift dough in parchment paper into the hot dutch oven and cover. 
8.  Bake 25 minutes covered. 
9.  Bake 20 minutes uncovered. 
10.  Cool on wire rack.
