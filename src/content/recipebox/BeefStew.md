## Ingredients
- chuck roast, cubed
- 1/2 c onion, chopped
Season with salt and pepper.  Brown the roast and saute the onions.  

- 1 clove garlic
- 7 c water
- 14 oz diced tomatoes
- 1/2 cup barley, uncooked
Simmer 1/2 hour

- 1/2 c celery
- 1/2 c sliced carrots
- 2 beef bullion cubes
- rutabaga (may substitute potato if needed)
- 1 bay leaf
Add hard veggies and simmer 45 minutes

- 1 bag frozen peas and corn
Add to taste and reheat until hot throughout.
