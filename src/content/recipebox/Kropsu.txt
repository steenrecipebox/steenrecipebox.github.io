This is such a tasty breakfast with some meat and fruit and yogurt and coffee! 

## Ingredients
- 1/2 stick butter
- 2 eggs (I use up to 4)
- 2 c flour
- 1/4 c sugar
- 2 c milk
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Instructions
1. Put butter in 9x12" pan and put in oven while it preheats to 400 degrees. 
2. Meanwhile, beat eggs with vanilla in large bowl. 
3. In small bowl, mix flour, sugar, salt. 
4. Mix dry ingredients alternately into eggs with milk (just so everything gets wet)
5. Pour into hot pan
6. Bake approx. 40 minutes.
