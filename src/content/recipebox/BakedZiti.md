## Ingredients
- 1 (60 oz) jar sauce (1 7/8 quarts)
- 1 (8 oz) pk cream cheese, cubed
- 1/2 tsp basil
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 - 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 lb. uncooked ziti
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella
- 3 tbsp grated parmesan
- Topper:  fresh basil
