## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked wild rice

Saute in 3 tbspn butter
- 1/4 cup onion, chopped
- 1 - 2 cloves garlic, diced
- 1/2 cup celery, chopped
- 1/2 cup red pepper, chopped
- 1/2 cup carrots, diced

Once veggies are tender, make roux by mixing then adding: 
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper 

Add: 
4+ cups chicken stock (heat to boil to thicken)
2 cups cooked chicken, diced
1 cup heavy cream (add slowly while stirring)

Optional: 
 - Can add 1/2 lb. bacon when sauteing vegetables
 - Can cook hole bag of wild rice and freeze in 2 cup increments for quick soup
