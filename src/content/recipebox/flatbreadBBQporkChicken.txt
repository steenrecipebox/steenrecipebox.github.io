## Ingredients
Saute before assembling on flatbread
- pork/chicken
- broccoli
- sun-dried tomatoes (or tomatoes/cherry)
- green bell peppers/yellow

## Instructions
1. Naan:  Bake 3 minutes at 400 degrees
2. Assemble toppings on naan.  
3. Drizzle with BBQ sauce and sprinkle with mozzarella
4. Bake 7 minutes at 400 degrees.  
5. Broil 3 minutes on high.
