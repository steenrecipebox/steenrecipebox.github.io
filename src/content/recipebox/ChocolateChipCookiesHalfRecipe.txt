## Ingredients
- 1 1/8 c whole wheat flour
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, softened
- 3/8 cup granulated sugar
- 3/8 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 large egg
- 1 cup chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup quick oats

Preheat oven to 375 degrees.
Bake 9-11minutes, sometimes longer

## Instructions
1. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a small bowl.  
2. Beat butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, and vanilla exract in large mixing bowl until creamy. 
3. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition.  
4.  Gradually eat in flour mixture. 
5.  Stir in chocolate and quick oats. 
6.  Drop by rounded tablespoon onto ungreased baking sheets.  
7.  Bake for 9 to 11 minutes or until golden brown.  
8.  Cool on baking sheets for 2 minutes.  
9.  Remove to wire racks to cool completely.
