# Roasted Chickpea Gyro Bowls
(Erin’s recipe, adapted from Pinterest recipe)
 
## Ingredients
- Two 15 oz cans chickpeas, rinsed
- 1/2 - 1 Tbsp olive oil

### Coating for chickpeas:
- 1/2 Tbsp paprika
o   1/2 tsp ground black pepper
o   1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
o   1/8 tsp salt

## Ingredients (Continued)
- 4 pita flatbreads (or naan)
- 1/4 red onion, cut into strips
- 2 lettuce leaves, roughly chopped (however much lettuce you enjoy)
- 1 tomato, sliced

### 2/3 cup – 1 cup tzatziki sauce
- 2/3 cup plain yogurt
- 1/3 cup cucumber, diced (or shredded)
- 2/3 Tbsp lemon juice (half lemon squeezed), (or white wine vinegar)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tsp dill, chopped (or dry)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions
1.  Preheat oven to 400°F.
2.  Rinse chickpeas, then pat dry with paper towel, removing any skins that may come off.
3.  Gently toss chickpeas with oil and coating (paprika, black pepper, cayenne pepper; salt)
4.  Spread chickpeas on a greased rimmed baking sheet.
5.  Prep quinoa – rinse, start in pot of water on stovetop. Cook 15-20 minutes.
6.  When over preheats, roast chickpeas for about 20 minutes until lightly browned but not hard (stir halfway through).
7.  Prep the remainder of the ingredients:
	a.  Cut onion, tomato, lettuce
	b.  Mix tzatziki sauce
8.  Assemble the Bowls!
	a. Put quinoa in bottom of bowls
	b. Add chickpeas
	c. Dish up remaining ingredients to liking:
        	i. 	Tzatziki sauce
        	ii. 	Onions
       		iii. 	Tomatoes
		iv. 	Lettuce
	d. Toast pita/naan for on the side
9.  Enioy!
